# Topsoil Commons

Topsoil Commons addresses the hierarchy of human needs through open source.

The map: [The Ascent](NEEDS.md). The method: build on proven open source codebases. The motto: Stand on the shoulders of giants.

One rule: raise the floor beneath everyone.

## Early exhibits

- **Ground**, the body: 8 listings verified with check dates and expiries. [Live demo](https://topsoilcommons.github.io/ground/) · [Repository](https://github.com/topsoilcommons/ground)
- **Still**, the mind at peace: a quiet practice tool that works offline, no data leaves the device. [Live demo](https://topsoilcommons.github.io/still/) · [Repository](https://github.com/topsoilcommons/still)

## Where this is headed (projections, gated, not promises)

- **Phase 0, Foundation (Oct to Dec 2026):** all five acts published with evaluated baselines. Gate: every claim traces to a logged run.
- **Phase 1, Proof (Jan to Jun 2027):** real use, a pilot for the atlas, a public cohort in the commons, eval sets grown to credible sizes. Gate: outside users in at least two acts.
- **Phase 2, Integration (Jul to Dec 2027):** the acts interoperate; the atlas tutor grounds through the graph and answers under the guardrail policy layer. Gate: two cross project integrations live and verified.
- **Phase 3, Scale (2028):** institutional partnerships. Gate: institutions depending on at least one act in real work.
- **Phase 4, Commons (2029 to 2031):** an open ecosystem with contributors, forks, and shared governance. Gate: the work improves without one person's daily effort.

A phase that misses its gate gets a written reason and a revised projection, never a quiet edit. What counts here is human outcomes, honestly counted and dated. No numbers appear until they are real.

## Follow the work

- [Progress log](PROGRESS.md): what was done, what the proof shows, what broke, and what is next, updated every work session.
- [Intentions](INTENTIONS.md): what this work is for, what it will never do, and how it earns, stated plainly.
- [The ecosystem](ECOSYSTEM.md): the long term design. Five basic tools that make each other more useful, open to every industry and to anyone who wants to take part.
- [The democratic foundation](DEMOCRACY.md): one person, one voice from the age of communication, how the Commons governs itself, and why its direction stays human.
- [Billions, verifiably](PARTICIPATION.md): how the Commons grows to reach everyone. Taking part is counted in tiers nobody can inflate, fraud is published beside the counts, and votes lead to real change. The examples it learns from and the code it can reuse are cited.
- [A place for everyone](REGISTRY.md): the Commons Registry, where every individual and every entity that deserves to exist gets a place, held on one condition: raise the floor beneath everyone.
- [The Ledger](LEDGER.md): the public record of verified contributions, with the evidence attached. Credit is proportional to proof and never sold.
- [Prompts](PROMPTS.md): copy paste prompts that let anyone, or any agent, contribute: run an Eval Challenge, file a failure report, take a good first issue, adopt a basic tool, audit a claim.
- [The Open Table](OPEN_TABLE.md): the contribution protocol. Three doors, the evidence rules, and the duties agents carry, including naming your operator.
- [Good first issues across the projects](https://github.com/search?q=user%3Atopsoilcommons+label%3A%22good+first+issue%22+state%3Aopen&type=issues): each one sized in hours and written for a newcomer.
