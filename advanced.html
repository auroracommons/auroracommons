Foundations: the studies as a path

Five findings can be run, each in under a minute:

  • False news travels faster

The long arc, with its sources

The timeline opens with the long arc: farming's ten independent origins and the milestones each harvest made possible. It stands at the head of The Record and in full in TIMELINE.md.

Every span here is a range because the scholarship gives ranges. These were slow processes, spread over centuries of trying, not single years with a date on them, and the beginnings are still argued over. Ethiopia's row is the least settled of the ten.

The research behind it opened 30 sources, peer reviewed papers, university publications, and museum and heritage records among them, and the notes name the argued points. Two claims were left out for lack of support, and the log says so: an Indus origin of farming, and an early date for enset. The entry stands in the progress log.

Where the fields meet

The Commons draws a few connections itself, between a finding in one field and the layer of need it speaks to. Each stands open to correction like everything else here.

  • Money and the body. The Kenya cash transfer finding, in Poverty and material security, measures the floor of the body directly: money with no conditions raised what households consumed. Run it. The tool at that layer is Ground.
  • Printed facts and shared truth. The Uganda report cards finding, in Governance and democracy, shows a community scorecard moving clinics and schools. Run it. Graph works the same bet at the layer of shared truth, and the Index files claims the same way, with their checks attached.
  • Help and learning. The study of AI help and novices, in Artificial intelligence and human computer interaction, found the least practiced gained the most from the same help. Run it. Atlas is built on that shape at the layer of learning: one claim at a time, its proof shown.
  • Speed and truth. False news travels faster than true news, in Truth and shared knowledge. Run the race. The Index exists in the shape of that finding: one timeline for everyone, claims before verdicts, corrections added and never quietly edited.
  • Ties and belonging. Connection and survival, in Belonging and social connection, ties the strength of a person's ties to living longer. Run it. Cohort serves that layer, and Still serves the mind that carries it.

Where the record disagrees with itself, both readings stand. In the Index, one early Graph figure stands filed as corrected, and its correction stands in its own entry beside it, each with its source and its hand. Nothing here is quietly edited; a fix is a new entry. See the pair in the Index.

Check it the way a scholar would

Every claim in this record carries three things: its ground, the source it stands on; its hand, the named person or people who stand behind it; and its check, the way a stranger can test it again and find the same result. The methods and measures stand one layer down from every claim, so a learner in any field can go as deep as the record goes.

  • The Index: its format, its six states, and its validator are kept in the Index repository, and the entries themselves stand in the viewer, each with its seal.
  • Atlas: one claim stands on its page with its full proof, the same on phone, watch, voice, and wall: the Proof screen.
  • Graph: its scores come from a hand labeled set of documents, and the misses are published beside the scores, in its repository and on its page.
  • Machine: its gradient checks and its two benchmark runs are recorded in its repository, with the numbers counted by the code.
  • Lumen: its refusal tests run over the real texts of its two exhibits, and the results stand in its repository.
  • Still: it collects nothing and calls nothing; its one measure, the pacer timing runs, stands committed in its repository, labeled as builder test runs.
  • Ground: every listing carries its check date and its expiry, and past expiry a listing shows as stale, never silently: the board and its repository.
