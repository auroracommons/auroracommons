# Aurora Commons: the timeline

These are intentions with gates, not promises. A phase begins only when the previous gate's published numbers hold.

## The iteration rule

Iterations are significant or they do not ship. Features are never stacked for their own sake. A phase adds depth where the evidence points, not breadth everywhere. Every expansion below names its trigger: the verified progress or the research finding that earns it. An expansion with no trigger does not appear.

## Mobile first, stated plainly

Every phase obeys the same interaction rules.

- Everything can be received and contributed from a phone, one handed.
- First value arrives inside a minute.
- Sessions are finite and end on purpose. The page says so.
- Nothing essential requires an account.
- Offline tolerance is the standard. Still's zero network page, which works with no connection at all, is the reference every other surface is measured against.
- Desktop and wall displays are renditions of the same record, never a richer truth.
- Contributions arrive through phone short forms, the way the doors work today: check a listing, check a claim, report an error, each in minutes, each credited.

## Phase 0: Prove. October to December 2026

Gate that opens Phase 1, reviewed December 28 to 31, 2026: all acts live with numbers refreshed at the December 14 freeze, and at least one of these true: an outside contributor has shipped, or an institution has held a real conversation naming a project. Intention at this gate: 1,000 citizens on the participation ladder, with the first participatory budget executed and published. This phase is the record itself, the baseline every later trigger is measured against.

- Ground (act at all): 13 places, 103 verified listings, each checked on the provider's own page with a date and an expiry.
- Still (choose): one page live in English; Spanish staged, awaiting a fluent human check; crisis lines verified for 11 of 12 countries.
- Atlas (understand): 344 topics, each with its sources.
- Cohort (contribute): a working room demonstrated with 25 members; its server cannot run on static hosting, and the record says so.
- Graph (discern): a hand labeled eval corpus of 20 documents; scores published beside the misses.
- Machine (build): two benchmarks run and recorded, on Shakespeare's sonnets and on Alice in Wonderland.
- Lumen (commit): two exhibits, each honored on its own terms; five Foundations demonstrations live.

## Phase 1: Invite. January to June 2027

Gate that opens Phase 2: outside users active in at least two acts, and one pilot running with a named institution.

- Ground: new places added by local adopters. Trigger: a first outside adopter keeps a board's checks current for a full cycle.
- Still: Spanish ships. Trigger: a fluent check resolves every disputed string in the staged translation, 13 today. Crisis lines extend only to countries verified the same way.
- Atlas: a first pilot in a school or district. Trigger: the Phase 0 gate's institution conversation naming Atlas.
- Cohort: a first real room, hosted, run by others. Trigger: a named community or company asks for the room in the open.
- Graph: the corpus grows. Trigger: claims arriving through the check a claim door, each passed by a second pair of eyes.
- Machine: the models become a teaching kit. Trigger: an outside party reruns the published benchmarks and gets the recorded numbers.
- Lumen: situations indexes begin. Trigger: the standing rule's second half, adopted October 15, 2026, which sets usefulness within each exhibit as the measure.

## Phase 2: Integrate. July to December 2027

Gate that opens Phase 3: at least two integrations live, and one institution has adopted a standard, an eval suite, a library, or an act.

- Ground: listings structured by Graph's extraction. Trigger: extraction scores on listing text, published and holding at the corpus standard.
- Still: its practices offered inside Cohort rooms. Trigger: a live outside room from Phase 1 asking for them.
- Atlas: content organized by Graph, tutor governed by Lumen's refusal policy. Trigger: the refusal suite passing on Atlas questions, run and published.
- Cohort: a portfolio builder room template. Trigger: two rooms run by outside groups, so the template copies practice, not hope.
- Graph: extraction runs on the Commons' own record. Trigger: the corpus past 20 documents with second checks logged.
- Machine: adopted primitives maintained as shared infrastructure. Trigger: a named adopter from Phase 1 depending on them.
- Lumen: the refusal suite used by outside builders. Trigger: an outside run on their own corpus, results published either way.

## Phase 3: Institutionalize. 2028

Gate that opens Phase 4: institutions depend on at least one act, and revenue covers operating costs.

- Ground: boards run by the towns themselves. Trigger: a town's own maintainers holding the check standard for two consecutive quarters.
- Still: languages multiply through community translators. Trigger: each version passing a fluent reader's check, the Spanish standard applied again.
- Atlas: adopted as a library by school networks. Trigger: the Phase 2 adoption, renewed by the institutions themselves.
- Cohort: rooms operated by institutions as their own commons. Trigger: rooms running a full year under the constitution without our hand.
- Graph: eval discipline offered as a service. Trigger: organizations paying for the discipline the eval record demonstrates.
- Machine: hosted primitives earn services income. Trigger: that income, published in the ledger, covering operating costs.
- Lumen: exhibits contributed by their own communities. Trigger: a community bringing its text forward under the standing rule.

Citizens intention this phase: 100,000, claimed only after two consecutive quarters at the published numbers.

## Phase 4: Commons. 2029 to 2031

Gate, the continuity test: the work runs for a full quarter without the founder's daily effort. That test is itself the trigger for everything in this phase.

- Ground: a federation of local boards, each keeping its own ledger, all readable as one record.
- Still: maintained by the people who use it, in their own languages.
- Atlas: external maintainers carry real ownership of the library.
- Cohort: local tables under one constitution, the global count a sum anyone can recompute.
- Graph: shared truth kept by many hands, corrections appended, never erased.
- Machine: the substrate runs whether or not any one builder is present.
- Lumen: exhibits across many traditions, none blended, none ranked.

Ten million is this phase's substrate intention. One billion and beyond comes only by federation, labeled intention until the counts hold for two consecutive quarters.

## What never changes

One rule: raise the floor beneath everyone. No blending and no ranking of traditions. Worth is never weighed; usefulness is measured within each exhibit, never across them. And every surface, in every phase, passes the delivery test: function first, value in the person's hands, and the sense that their time was well spent.
