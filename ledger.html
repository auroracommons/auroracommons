The Ledger: Verified Contributions

Source: LEDGER.md, kept on GitHub.

Part of the Record: the written record of Aurora Commons, on one page each.

Opened October 15, 2026. This is the public record of who built what in this ecosystem, with the evidence attached. The rules, stated once and binding on every entry: an entry exists only for verified work (a merged pull request, a published run, a documented adoption); credit is proportional to what the evidence shows; credit is never sold or traded; and anyone can check any entry against its evidence link. The full terms are in OPEN_TABLE.md on this profile.

Every entry to date is the founder and agents working under his direction. That is the honest starting state of any young project. The table is now open; the entries that matter most are the ones that come next.

How an entry gets made

When a contribution merges or a verified adoption is reported, an entry is added here in the same change or the next progress update: date, contributor (person, organization, or agent with its operator named), what was built, and the evidence link. Disputed entries are resolved in the open, in this file's history, where anyone can watch.
