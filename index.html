Ways in

  • Ground and Still, if you need help today. Food, shelter, and health help near you, each listing with its check date; and a quiet tool to steady your mind.
  • Come in, if you want to contribute. Every act states its minutes and what it changes, and lands in the public ledger under your name, a stage name, or no name, as you choose.
  • Public records, if you run an organization or a government. What stands in the open for every need, the governance documents, and the registry. A place is given, never bought.
  • Funds, if you support or fund work like this. The intended host is Open Source Collective; an application was submitted on October 16, 2026 and is under review; no host is appointed and no funds have been received. Money moves only through the host, never to a personal account.
  • The Index, if you check claims. Every entry a claim with its ground, its hand, and its check, in time order; recompute a seal yourself and file what you get.
