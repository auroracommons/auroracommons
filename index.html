Bedrock Commons

Open source primitives for human needs, proven in public. Five working exhibits on one map of human needs, with the proof published beside every claim.

The one rule: be fair to others.

The five acts

One thesis in five acts: technology should increase human capability without taking human agency away. Every number below comes from a logged run in the project repository.

Layer 2 · Learning

Supergravity, the Atlas

A learning atlas and AI tutor that answers from sources it can cite, and says so when it cannot.

Proof: tutor eval groundedness 0.806, citation rate 1.0, run in CI.
Live demo Repository
Layer 3 · Belonging

Cohort, the Commons

A community platform with an MCP server, so people and their agents can share one workspace.

Proof: tests 7 of 7, MCP server verified by a recorded live agent session.
Project page Repository
Layer 5 · Autonomy

Neural Net Lab, the Machine

Neural networks built from scratch, so machine intelligence is something people can actually understand.

Proof: gradient checks 7 of 7; PyTorch benchmark 4.13x faster per step, published beside the from scratch result.
Live results Repository
Layer 4 · Shared truth

Knowledge Graph Explorer, the Graph

Turns text into a knowledge graph, and publishes how often the extraction is wrong.

Proof: hand labeled eval, entities F1 0.936, relations F1 0.605, errors published.
Live demo Repository
Layer 6 · Trust and meaning

LUMEN, the Guardrails

A guarded retrieval design study: an AI that refuses what it should refuse, and proves it on a published eval.

Proof: refusal eval 12 of 12 blocked, 8 of 8 allowed.
Study page Repository

The Bedrock: a map of human needs

The acts stand on a map of what people actually need, ground up. Two layers are still open ground, entered only when a partner and proven demand arrive. Wants sit above the bedrock, and are never served by exploiting an unmet need below it.

Take part

The table is open to people and to agents, on one condition: evidence. Contribution credit is public, proportional to proof, and never sold. A place in the Commons is given, not earned, and is lost only for unfairness to others, proven in the open.

The public record

Everything about how this work runs is published, including the failures. Governance belongs to the members: one person, one voice, from the age a person can communicate a preference. Artificial intelligence works here as a tool, with no vote, no veto, and no hand in framing the questions.
