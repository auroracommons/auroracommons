The Proof

The building block of this Commons. One claim, bound to its evidence, held by a named human, carrying its own check.

Claimone sentence, plain words
A Proofcheckable by anyone
Groundthe evidence it stands on
Handthe human who stands behind it
Checkverified, corrections appended
Linkswhat it supports and contradicts
