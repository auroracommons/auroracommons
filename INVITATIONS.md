# Invitations: A Map of Doors

Written October 15, 2026. This map lists public contact routes for organizations the Commons would one day invite to its open table.

No relationship is claimed with any organization listed here. This is a map of doors, not a list of partners. Invited means invited, never involved, until an organization's own record says otherwise. No one was contacted to make this map, and nothing was sent, submitted, or promised to anyone on it.

## How to read this map

Each entry names the organization, the layer of the Ascent or the piece of Commons work it touches, and one public contact route. Every route was checked on the organization's own site on October 15, 2026. A route that could not be found on the organization's own site was not listed.

The layers are named in NEEDS.md, the Ascent: 0 the body, 1 the mind at peace, 2 learning, 3 belonging, 4 shared truth, 5 autonomy, 6 trust and meaning.

## Food logistics

- **Feeding America.** Touches Layer 0, the body, and Ground's listings, through a national network of food banks moving food to local pantries. Public route: [Contact page](https://www.feedingamerica.org/about-us/contact)
- **City Harvest.** Touches Layer 0, the body, and Ground's listings, through food rescue and delivery in New York City. Public route: [Contact page](https://www.cityharvest.org/contact-us/)

## Libraries

- **The New York Public Library.** Touches Layer 2, learning, and Layer 3, belonging, through free library service across the Bronx, Manhattan, and Staten Island. Public route: [Contact page](https://www.nypl.org/get-help/contact-us)
- **Brooklyn Public Library.** Touches Layer 2, learning, and Layer 3, belonging, through free library service across Brooklyn. Public route: [Contact page](https://www.bklynlibrary.org/contact)

## Crisis lines

- **988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline.** Touches Layer 1, the mind at peace, and Still, whose crisis block points people to real help. Public route: [Contact page](https://988lifeline.org/contact-us/)
- **The Trevor Project.** Touches Layer 1, the mind at peace, and Still, through crisis support for young people. Public route: [Contact page](https://www.thetrevorproject.org/contact-us/)

## Community foundations

- **The New York Community Trust.** Touches Layer 3, belonging, and the Ascent as a whole, through local grantmaking across New York City, Long Island, and Westchester. Public route: [Contact page](https://thenytrust.org/contact/), general inquiries at info@thenytrust.org
- **The Westchester Community Foundation.** Touches Layer 0, the body, and Ground's listings, through local grantmaking in Westchester, the county where Ground's seed board stands. Public route: [Contact page](https://wcf-ny.org/about/contact/), general inquiries at info@wcf-ny.org

## Open source foundations

- **The Linux Foundation.** Touches Layer 5, autonomy, through open source projects people can study, use, and fork. Public route: [Contact page](https://www.linuxfoundation.org/about/contact)
- **The Apache Software Foundation.** Touches Layer 5, autonomy, and Layer 4, shared truth, through open source projects and the public record of how they are built. Public route: [Contact page](http://apache.org/foundation/contact)

## Universities

- **Fordham University.** Touches Layer 2, learning. The route listed is its undergraduate admission contact page, the public door for new students. Public route: [Contact page](https://www.fordham.edu/undergraduate-admission/contact-us/)
- **New York University.** Touches Layer 2, learning. Public route: [Contact page](https://www.nyu.edu/en/footer/contact-us.html)

## The check

Twelve routes are listed, two in each of six groups. Four candidates were dropped because a public contact route could not be verified on the organization's own site. If a route above has moved, a correction with the new address on the organization's own site is welcome in this repository's issues.
