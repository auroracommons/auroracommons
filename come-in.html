About money

Money is one act among many, never the headline. It moves only through a fiscal host, never to a personal account, and every dollar lands in a public ledger. That door opens when the host is appointed. Until then it stays shut, and this page says so rather than taking money it cannot yet hold in the open.

What opens, the day a host is appointed: giving once or monthly on the host's own hosted page, reached from this site by one setting changed, not by anything new built. The host receives and holds the money, and the gift stands in the public ledger within seven days of the host's statement. The staged endpoint already answers at topsoilcommons.org/api/give, saying in the open that the door is shut and why.

Keep your own listing true. Your hours and address, current, checked by you. About 10 minutes. It is the cheapest true fact this system has, and it is credited like anyone's check. When you are ready, run your own board on the same open standard and join it to the shared record.

Ways to contribute

Every act states its time and what it changes. Each one lands in the public ledger, under your name, a stage name, or no name, as you choose. No act buys a vote, a rank, or a place. How participation is counted, in the open.

  1. Check a listing. 3 minutes. Its check date moves to today, and it stays true 30 more days.
  2. Add a missing listing. 5 minutes. A new source of help enters the board, marked as proposed until a second person checks it.
  3. Run one finding, then say where it lost you. 3 minutes. Your note makes the next version clearer.
  4. Check a claim. 5 minutes. Recompute a proof and file what you get. A failed check is a contribution too. It becomes a correction, never a secret.
  5. Witness a moment. 5 minutes. File it to the Index with its time and place, under your hand.
  6. Come to the common hour. 1 hour a week. New entries checked together, in the open.
  7. Report an error. 1 minute. Anything wrong, anywhere in the record.
  8. Translate one page. Your pace. Nothing ships until a fluent person checks it, and the checker is credited.
  9. Keep a place. A little, often. Hold your town's board and timeline true. This is the rung the whole thing leans on.

The ladder, in one line: use it, check one thing, add one thing, witness with others, keep a place, govern together. Every rung is open to everyone from the first day.
