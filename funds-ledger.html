Source: FUNDS_LEDGER.md, kept on GitHub.

Part of the Record: the written record of Topsoil Commons, on one page each.

Opened October 15, 2026. This is the public record of every amount Topsoil Commons receives and spends. The rules, stated once and binding on every entry: money moves only through the fiscal host, never into a personal account; every amount is entered here with its date, its source or its purpose, and its amount; and every entry reconciles to the host's own statement, so any stranger can check it. The full rules are in Funds on this profile.
