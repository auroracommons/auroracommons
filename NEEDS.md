# The Bedrock: A Map of Human Needs

Written October 15, 2026. This is the foundation document of the ecosystem. Every project in this work exists to serve a need named here, and this map names the whole territory, including the ground no project covers yet. A need is not covered because it is mentioned. It is covered when working proof serves it, and this map marks the difference in plain sight.

## Why a map of needs

Hate and harm grow where needs have no legitimate path. So the work starts at the foundation: what does a human being actually need, in the order the needs hold each other up? Everything built here stands on this. Bedrock first, then the house.

The bedrock is layered because needs are layered. A person who is hungry and unsafe cannot study. A person who is fed but utterly alone is not well. A person surrounded by people but drowning in falsehood cannot choose freely. Read the layers from the ground up. Each one rests on the ones beneath it, and each project in the ecosystem serves the layer it stands on.

And beneath the layers, the grain: every need on this map is lived by one person, on one ordinary day, in one kitchen, one classroom, one night shift. Sand is the truth of scale. Millions of single grains, each one a person. Work that forgets the grain becomes abstraction. Work that forgets the bedrock becomes noise. This ecosystem holds both.

## The layers

### Layer 0: The body. Food, shelter, health, safety.
The material floor. Reliable food, a safe place to sleep, a body that is cared for, a life not lived in fear of violence or ruinous bills.
Unmet, it looks like: hunger inside a system that wastes food by the ton, families one emergency from the street, treatable illness untreated.
Served by: no act yet. This is named honestly as open ground.
The path in: the ecosystem enters by adoption, the same road as every industry. A food logistics team adopts the Graph to structure what is wasted and what is needed. A community health educator adopts the Atlas to teach. The gate is a real partner running a real pilot with measured results, the Phase 1 and Phase 2 gates in the strategy. Until that evidence exists, this layer is a commitment with a road, not a claim.

### Layer 1: The mind at peace. Mental health, steadiness, hope.
A mind that can rest. Treatment and support for suffering, freedom from constant dread, and hope treated as infrastructure: a credible path, proof someone like you walked it, a first step small enough to take today.
Unmet, it looks like: despair, addiction, isolation that hardens into contempt for self and others.
Served by: partly, and thinly. Cohort serves it through belonging. LUMEN serves it through meaning. The public record serves it through visible paths and kept promises.
Status: partial. No act is dedicated to it, and this map does not pretend otherwise. A dedicated act earns its place only through the same gates as everything else: proven demand, a partner, measured results.

### Layer 2: Learning. Education and growing capability.
The ability to learn what you need, when you need it, at a price that does not exclude you, from teaching that does not invent its facts.
Unmet, it looks like: students priced out of the tutoring that changes trajectories, adults locked out of retraining, knowledge hoarded behind paywalls and jargon.
Served by: Act 1, Supergravity, the Atlas. A free learning atlas whose tutor is evaluated in public, groundedness 0.806 and citation rate 1.0 on the committed baseline, with the weak spots published beside the scores.
Proof standard: the eval harness runs in the open and anyone can rerun it.

### Layer 3: Belonging. Connection, community, being known.
People who know your name. A room where your work is seen. A first believer when you are starting from nothing.
Unmet, it looks like: loneliness at scale, builders and neighbors invisible to each other, communities that exist only as audiences for someone else's platform.
Served by: Act 2, Cohort, the Commons. Workspaces where people and AI agents build together in the open, through the Model Context Protocol, with sessions anyone can watch instead of black boxes.
Proof standard: live sessions and the public record of what was built in them.

### Layer 4: Shared truth. Facts a community can stand on.
Knowledge organized well enough to argue with. Claims that carry their evidence. Errors published, not buried.
Unmet, it looks like: decisions made about communities from scattered and invented data, arguments with no floor of agreed fact, institutions that cannot tell what they know from what they assume.
Served by: Act 4, Knowledge Graph Explorer, the Graph. Extraction that publishes its own scores, entities F1 0.937 and relations F1 0.605 on a hand labeled eval, with the misses printed next to the numbers.
Proof standard: the eval set is public and the extraction can be rerun against it.

### Layer 5: Autonomy. Not ruled by machines you cannot understand.
The machinery of the age opened up, so that understanding is not the property of a priesthood. A society that cannot explain its own machines will be governed by whoever can.
Unmet, it looks like: whole populations subject to systems they cannot question, skills and standing allocated by black boxes, dependence dressed up as convenience.
Served by: Act 3, Neural Net Lab, the Machine. Machine learning built from scratch, by hand, in the open: a transformer with 7 of 7 gradient checks passing and an honest benchmark against PyTorch, so anyone can learn how the machine truly learns.
Proof standard: the code is the curriculum, and its tests are the receipts.

### Layer 6: Trust and meaning. Safety, values, a reason.
Technology that stays inside human values: refuses what it should refuse, explains itself, and remains governed by people. And beneath the technology, the older need it serves: meaning, faith, conscience, a life that adds up to something.
Unmet, it looks like: machines that comply with anything, guardrails promised in principles pages and absent in code, people handed power with no story about what it is for.
Served by: Act 5, LUMEN, the Guardrails. Guarded question answering over a public domain corpus, with a refusal evaluation anyone can rerun: 12 of 12 harmful requests blocked, 8 of 8 legitimate ones allowed, in the committed run.
Proof standard: the refusal suite is reusable and public, so the boundary can be tested by anyone, not trusted on our word.

## Above the bedrock: wants

Needs are the floor. Wants are the life built on it: recognition for real work, achievement, play, beauty, wealth honestly earned, the standing that comes from contribution. The ecosystem serves wants through the same primitives, never by manufacturing desire or steering behavior in the dark. The contribution ledger exists for recognition. The open table exists for achievement. The rule is simple: wants are served in the open, on top of met needs, and never by exploiting the unmet needs underneath.

## The whole story in one breath

A person stands on material security, steadied in mind, able to learn, belonging to people, standing on shared facts, free from machines they cannot question, inside technology that respects their values, with a path in front of them small enough to start today. That is the complete human picture this ecosystem serves. Five acts cover five layers with working proof today. Two layers, the body and the mind at peace, are named as open ground with roads in and gates on the roads. Nothing on this map is claimed before it is proven, and the map itself will change only when the evidence changes it.

## How this map governs the projects

Every project states the layer it serves at the top of its own front door, defines that need in the words of this map, and links back here. Every proposed addition to the ecosystem names its layer and passes the three tests: the charity test, the love test, and the needs check. A project that cannot name its layer does not join. A claim that outruns its proof is corrected in the open, the way it has been from the first night.
